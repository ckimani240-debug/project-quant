# PROJECT QUANT
# Trading Research Standards

Version: 1.0
Status: Foundational
Initial markets: BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT

## 1. Mission

Project Quant aims to develop a rigorous, evidence-driven
cryptocurrency market research and decision-support system.

Its priorities are:
1. Capital preservation.
2. Reliable market analysis.
3. Measurable trading advantages.
4. Consistent risk management.
5. Transparent and explainable decisions.

Profitability must never be assumed.

## 2. Research Sources

Prioritise:
- First-person trader interviews.
- Recorded lectures and presentations.
- Verified interview transcripts.
- Published books and research papers.
- Reputable financial institutions.
- Original quantitative research.

Every source must record:
- Author or speaker.
- Title.
- Publication or recording date.
- Publisher or interviewer.
- Original URL or reference.
- Date accessed.
- Relevant quotation or paraphrase.
- Context and limitations.

Never invent quotations or attribute unsupported
strategies to traders.

## 3. Trading Principles

Every extracted principle must include:
- Principle name.
- Original source.
- Explanation.
- Trading context.
- Potential application to cryptocurrency.
- Potential limitations.
- Proposed implementation.
- Testing status.

Distinguish direct quotations from paraphrases.

## 4. Hypothesis Development

Every proposed strategy must have:
- A clearly defined hypothesis.
- Entry conditions.
- Exit conditions.
- Stop-loss rules.
- Position-sizing rules.
- Intended timeframe.
- Required market data.
- Expected behaviour.
- Conditions under which it should not trade.

A hypothesis is not a proven trading edge.

## 5. Data Integrity

Project Quant must:
- Use verifiable market data.
- Exclude incomplete candles when required.
- Prevent look-ahead bias.
- Prevent data leakage.
- Handle missing and invalid observations.
- Record timestamps and candle intervals.
- Preserve historical data provenance.

Never fabricate market prices or trading results.

## 6. Backtesting Standards

Every experiment must account for:
- Trading fees.
- Slippage.
- Position sizing.
- Stop-loss execution assumptions.
- Market exposure.
- Drawdown.
- Out-of-sample performance.

Where appropriate, compare strategies against
buy-and-hold and a no-trade benchmark.

Use walk-forward validation where practical.

Avoid optimising parameters solely to improve
historical results.

## 7. Risk Management

Initial project limits:

- Virtual starting balance: 1,000 USDT.
- Maximum portfolio exposure: 50%.
- Maximum risk per trade: 1%.
- Maximum daily simulated loss: 3%.
- Drawdown pause threshold: 15%.
- Trading fees: 0.1% per side.
- Slippage assumption: 0.05% per side.

These are initial research settings, not guarantees
of safety or profitability.

Risk controls must not be bypassed by strategies.

## 8. Market Sessions

Analyse relevant market activity across:
- Asian session.
- London session.
- New York session.
- Major session overlaps.

Account for daylight-saving changes.

Cryptocurrency markets operate 24 hours a day.
Sessions are analytical periods, not exchange
opening hours.

Session analysis must be performed on demand.

## 9. Experimental Validation

Every experiment must record:
- Experiment identifier.
- Hypothesis.
- Data period.
- Market and timeframe.
- Strategy parameters.
- Transaction-cost assumptions.
- Training results.
- Out-of-sample results.
- Benchmark comparison.
- Limitations.
- Final conclusion.

Possible conclusions:

- ACCEPTED FOR FURTHER TESTING
- REJECTED
- INCONCLUSIVE
- REQUIRES MORE DATA

No strategy enters the recommendation engine
without documented validation.

## 10. Decision Standards

Project Quant must be able to recommend:

- BUY
- SELL
- WAIT
- NO TRADE

Every recommendation must explain:
- Supporting evidence.
- Conflicting evidence.
- Market conditions.
- Entry conditions.
- Invalidation level.
- Stop-loss.
- Position size.
- Estimated costs.
- Risk-to-reward ratio.
- Important uncertainties.

A NO TRADE decision is a valid outcome.

## 11. Operational Safety

- Public market data initially.
- No real-money execution.
- No leverage, margin or futures.
- Explicit approval for every simulated trade.
- No background monitoring.
- No automatic trading.
- Preserve local portfolio records.
- Never expose API credentials or secrets.

## 12. Continuous Improvement

Project Quant must evaluate its own performance.

A strategy that previously worked may stop working.

Monitor:
- Changes in market conditions.
- Performance deterioration.
- Drawdown.
- Transaction costs.
- Model stability.
- Out-of-sample performance.

Never automatically assume a strategy remains
effective indefinitely.

## 13. Final Principle

Evidence before confidence.
Risk management before opportunity.
Validation before deployment.

Project Quant must never promise profits.
